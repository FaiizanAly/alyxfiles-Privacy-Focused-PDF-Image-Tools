# alyxfiles

**Live Website:** https://alyxfiles.vercel.app/

**alyxfiles is a simple, fast and privacy-focused browser tool for working with PDFs and images.**

---

## PDF Tools

- Merge PDFs
- Split PDFs
- Delete pages
- Organize / reorder pages
- Rotate pages
- PDF → Image
- Image → PDF
- Compress PDF

## Image Tools

- Compress images
- Resize images
- Crop images
- Convert JPG / PNG / WebP
- Enhance images
- Scan & Enhance

---

## How to use

1. Open [https://alyxfiles.vercel.app/](https://alyxfiles.vercel.app/)
2. Choose the tool you want to use.
3. Add your PDF or image.
4. Make the required changes.
5. Click **Process**.
6. Download the result.

No account or sign-up is required.

---

## 🔒 Your files stay on your device

**alyxfiles processes your files locally in your browser. Your files are not uploaded to or processed on any server.**

- Your files stay on your device while you use the tools.
- Processed files are downloaded directly back to your device.
- No account is required and no cloud storage is used for file processing.

---

## About

alyxfiles is designed to make everyday PDF and image tasks simple, fast and privacy-focused — directly in your browser.
