Privacy by design.

Your files stay on your device.
alyxfiles processes files directly in your browser. Files are not uploaded to our servers.

Files stay local

alyxfiles processes supported files directly inside your browser. Files are not uploaded to an alyxfiles server for processing.

No account required

No account is required. No cloud storage is used for the file-processing workflow. Open the site and use it immediately.

Direct download

Processed files are downloaded directly to your device. Nothing is stored on any server.

Browser memory only

While a tool is running, your file data lives temporarily in browser memory and is cleared when you close or leave the page.
